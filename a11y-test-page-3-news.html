City Council Approves New Transit Budget Amid Public Debate

By Staff Reporter | Published 2 hours ago | Updated 10 minutes ago

The city council voted 6-3 Tuesday night to approve a $12 million increase to the regional transit budget, following months of public hearings and contentious debate over funding priorities.

Supporters of the measure argued that aging infrastructure required immediate investment, while opponents raised concerns about the impact on the general fund.

The new budget takes effect at the start of the next fiscal quarter and includes provisions for expanded bus routes in underserved neighborhoods.

"This is the most significant investment in public transit in a generation," said one council member.
